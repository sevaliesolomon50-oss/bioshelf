START LEARNING TODAY

Three ideas worth knowing.

01 / BASICS

What is biology?

Biology is the scientific study of life and living organisms, including their structure, function, growth, evolution, and relationships with their environments.

02 / CELLS

What is a cell?

A cell is the smallest structural and functional unit of an organism. Some organisms consist of one cell, while others are made of many cells.

03 / LIFE

How do we study life?

Biologists investigate processes such as energy use, growth, response to stimuli, reproduction, and how organisms interact with their surroundings.
